Domain palette

Nine hues for the seven seeded stewardship domains plus two spares. Six were chosen — Green, Blue, Indigo and Yellow from Apple's system colours, Orchid and Rose ours — and three were computed to fill what those six left open. Then all nine were measured: every pair has to stay apart at 9px, and none of them may wander into the band the accent and the priority ring already own. Travel is the one exemption from the 3:1 contrast floor, so the yellow can be a full yellow.

The shapes a domain colour takes

A 9px dot beside a row title, a filled block on the day tape, an outlined tick for a scheduled task, and a 28px swatch in the picker. All four, both themes, real sizes.

Separation at dot size

Pairwise perceptual distance in OKLab (ΔEok). A 9px dot is a small field, so the floor here is 0.12 — well above the ~0.02 just-noticeable difference for large areas. Anything under 0.12 is flagged; under 0.09 is a defect.

Against what is already spoken for

The accent (#f15a0f) marks interactivity and overdue; the priority ring (#d92d20) marks a high-priority task. A domain colour that lands near either one lies about what it is. Distance from every domain to both, same ΔEok scale, floor 0.15 — higher, because confusing a domain with a state is worse than confusing two domains.
