2 · Tape is shape; start times above works

The block only has to stay visible and true to its position, which a 7px minimum guarantees. Start times ride above the track where width costs nothing, and every title is already one line below in the Timeline list. Nothing is lost and nothing can truncate.

3 · Titles outside, staggered below works, costs height

Your "put the title on the outside" idea. Labels hang off each block's left edge on two alternating rows so neighbours don't collide. It reads well on a quiet day and it is the first thing to break on a busy one — five morning meetings and the rows overlap again, needing a third row or dropped labels.
