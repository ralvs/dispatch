Gate B — the rhythm rule

DESIGN.md says 16px between cards, 34–40px between sections, 64px under the header. One call site in the app is inside the band. The tree is an undocumented three-tier: ~32px on list pages, ~56px between major detail blocks, 24px in a couple of tight stacks. "64px under the header" is also ambiguous — that is AppHeader's mb-16, not PageHeader's own bottom margin.

Gate A is held at B (counts only) below so the two questions do not fight. Gap tags are lab chrome — they name the margin in px.

B0 As shipped — three values, no name

What the tree does. List groups at 32, detail major blocks at 56, Details first section at 32. Three first:mt-* cancellations exist because every page owns its own top margin.

mt-8 = 32 · mt-14 = 56 · mt-6 = 24 · first:mt-0 / first:mt-2 hacks

3 Either of the above + a module that owns it

A PageBody (or the authed layout) applies one gap / space-y to the section stack. Sections carry no top margin — so the first-child case cannot arise and all three first:mt-* cancellations disappear.

Cost: every page that stacks sections migrates into the wrapper. Mix of list-tier and major-tier on one page needs either two wrappers or a per-section tier prop — drawn here as two bodies with a single gap each (list 32 / detail 56), matching option 2's numbers. Pair with option 1 by using 36 on both.

Parent: display:flex; flex-direction:column; gap:…
Children: margin-top: 0. No first-child rules.
