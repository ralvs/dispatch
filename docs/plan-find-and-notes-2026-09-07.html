Find, and two note instruments

Three things, one pass. A way to find a task — in its title or its notes — or a sentence inside a note. A Grok-style section widget on the left of a note: equal ticks, a hover pill, chevrons. Colour on a selected word, from the app palette, including orange, yellow and red.

This is a plan, not a build. Revised after the owner’s Grok screenshots and three corrections: Find must read tasks.notes; ink may use accent / yellow / red inside a note; the tape is Grok’s tick widget, not a proportional minimap.

01 Three verbs, already almost there

The shell already has Capture (⌘J, filled pill) and Ask (the outlined pill to /chat). Find is the missing third verb. It does not get a pill. DESIGN.md spent the two standing actions on purpose; a third one would make Capture ordinary.

Capture · ⌘J
A thought in. The plus. Persist first, parse second. Already the product.
Find · ⌘K
A thing that already exists. Tasks and notes. This plan.
Ask · /chat
A question over a snapshot. Read-only. Stays that. Find locates; Ask talks.
Why Find is not Chat. Chat answers “what did I say about the dentist.” Find answers “where is the dentist.” Mixing them makes both worse: a locate tool that writes prose, and a reader that you have to wait on to open a row. The owner already has both jobs. They get two doors.

02 Find

A command palette over the two piles you actually lose things in. The link picker already searches task titles with ilike. Find is not that function with a new name. It reads four fields: task title, task notes, note title, note body.

The scene

At the keyboard. You remember a word, not a route. You press ⌘K. You type dentista. Two groups appear: Tasks, then Notes. A title match sits above a notes/body match. A hit inside task notes or a note body shows a one-line snippet with the word marked. Enter opens the row — /tasks?edit= or /notes/[id]. Escape closes. The composer never creates anything.

On a phone there is no ⌘K. Find sits in the More menu, Daily section, next to Ask. Same palette, same action, opened from the menu instead of a key.

What “smart” is, and what it is not

The gap today is that nothing reads notes.body or tasks.notes. The link picker looks at task titles only — searchTasksByTitle stays as it is, for the link picker. Find is a new service with an or on both columns. Reusing the title-only helper would drop every match that lives in the task’s notes field, which is the thing the owner asked to keep.

Smart, here, is ranking and a snippet:

  • A title hit outranks a notes/body hit. Always. You named it; that is the thing.
  • Inside a group, open tasks sit above done; needs_review notes sit above the rest. Then recency.
  • A notes/body hit carries ~80 characters around the first match, markdown stripped, the query marked in accent-ink. The stored text is never mutated. The meta line names the field: “in the task notes” or “in the body.”
  • Quiet tasks still appear. Find is not Today. Parking a project must not hide the row from a search for its words.
  • Two characters minimum. Empty query shows recents: five open tasks, five recent notes. A blank box is a dead control.
  • Cap eight per group. A palette is not a search-engine page.

Not smart: embeddings, a second model, typo-tolerant trigrams, “search everything.” pg_trgm is a later index if ILIKE ever feels slow, which a single-user corpus will not. Journal, quotes, people, links, events, the notification ledger — named as a growth path, not built. Searching “Ana” already hits a task whose title or notes contain Ana; mentions do not need a second index.

How it is built

One server action behind requireOwner(). One service, lib/services/find.ts, that queries tasks and notes in parallel. Tasks: title.ilike or notes.ilike. Notes: title.ilike or body.ilike. Same escaped ilike the link picker already uses, on more columns. Ranking is applied in process on the small cap, not in SQL. No generated tsvector column. No migration. Bilingual content is stored verbatim; ilike is case-insensitive and language-agnostic, which is the honest choice for mixed PT-BR/EN. An English FTS config would stem the wrong language.

The palette is a sibling of CapturePalette: a bus, a shortcut helper, a Dialog, mounted once in the authed shell. ⌘J stays Capture. ⌘K is Find. They must not steal each other’s key. Debounce 150ms on the client so a fast typist does not fire six round-trips.

03 The section ticks

The first draft of this plan drew a proportional minimap: long section, long mark, heading depth as inset. That is not the widget. The owner’s Grok screenshots are the spec. Copy that instrument. Put it on the left of the note, because the right is already the links rail.

What the screenshots actually are

At rest: a vertical stack of short equal dashes on the far edge, one per section. No labels. No chevrons. Quiet ink-4 lines, a couple of pixels thick, maybe 16px wide. The current section is a slightly brighter dash, not a different shape.

On hover of the stack: chevrons appear above and below, and the dash under the pointer grows a rounded pill to its side with a snippet of that section — in Grok, the first line of the turn. Click the dash or the pill, that section comes to you. Click a chevron, previous / next section.

It is an overlay in the margin. It does not take a grid column. It does not scale with section length. It does not indent by heading level. Those three were the last draft, and they are out.

What a “section” is, on a note

Grok’s unit is a chat turn. A note’s unit is a heading: the title, then every # / ## / ### in the body. The pill shows that heading’s text, truncated. A note with no headings has one tick (the title) and the widget hides — a stack of one is not a navigator.

It still hides when the whole note fits in the viewport. Chevrons with nowhere to go are chrome.

Left, overlay, not a column

Grok parks this on the right because a chat has empty margin there. A Dispatch note already spends that margin on the 260px rail (ADR-0047). The widget sits in the left padding of the note column, overlay, sticky with the editor. It must not shrink the 65ch. On a phone the same overlay: dashes only, tap jumps, no hover pill (there is no hover). Chevrons may appear while a finger is down on the stack.

How it is built

A client companion of NoteEditor. On update, collect the title plus every heading node. Equal ticks, in document order, not mapped to rendered height. Scroll-spy against #main (the shell is the scroller, ADR-0028) lights the current dash. Click / chevron calls scrollIntoView. Hover of a dash opens the pill. prefers-reduced-motion jumps, it does not smooth-scroll.

The stack is a list of links with aria-label="Note sections". The pill is role="tooltip". Keyboard users already have the headings in the body; the ticks are a pointing-device shortcut and stay out of the long tab path.

04 Colour on a word

Select a word, apply one of the colours the app already speaks. No picker, no hex, no highlight-marker. The stored value is a palette slug so light and dark both resolve it, the same way a domain dot already does.

The set

The first draft kept chrome colours out of the note: accent is overdue, yellow fails contrast, red is an error. The owner’s correction: that collision is a chrome problem. Inside a note, a word is not a button. Put them in.

Eleven slugs, all existing tokens, no hex, no picker:

Orange · accent Red · error Yellow · travel Indigo · engine Green · health Orchid · family Clay · spirit Blue · finance Cyan · code Teal · pine Rose · burgundy

A coloured word is not a domain tag and is not overdue. Colouring a sentence orange does not make a task slip. Filing stays the ScopeSelect under the editor. Yellow on light ground will be bright; that is accepted for this surface.

Storage, and why markdown stays

ADR-0009 stores the body as markdown text, serialized by tiptap-markdown with html: false. That rule stands. Colour is one inline mark with a slug attribute. On the way out it writes:

<span data-ink="health">dentista</span>

On the way in, only a span[data-ink] whose value is one of the eleven slugs becomes a mark. Any other HTML stays text. Unknown slugs strip to plain. Hex, style=, <font> — gone. Capture can still dump a raw angle bracket into a note and it will show as a bracket, not as markup.

This is the same escape hatch empty paragraphs already use (&nbsp; in, empty <p> out). We are not turning the editor into an HTML store. We are naming one tag the serializer may emit, and we are writing that down in an amendment to ADR-0009 so the next reader does not treat it as drift.

The bubble

The editor has no toolbar today. Markdown shortcuts own bold, italic, lists, headings. That does not change. Colour has no markdown shortcut worth teaching, so it appears only when the selection is non-empty: a TipTap BubbleMenu of eleven swatches plus clear. Reuse the swatch language from ColorSwatchPicker — same size, same ring, same labels — in a compact row. Applying a colour to a selection that already has one replaces it. Clear removes the mark and the word returns to ink.

iOS selection handles and the bubble will fight; that is a known TipTap tax. The bubble must sit above the selection, not over the native handles, and it must not steal the first tap. If that cannot be made decent on a phone, colour is a desk feature and the bubble does not mount below lg. That fallback is allowed. Inventing a second colour UI for the phone is not.

07 Constraints the build may not invent past

Still true

  • Iron rules 1–6. Find is a read, so #6 does not fire. Colour is user-authored, so #5 (verbatim) still holds — we store the words, plus a slug.
  • requireOwner() on the Find action. No /api/find.
  • Services take sb first.
  • UI chrome English. Content in the language it was written.
  • Semantic tokens only. No raw hex in components.
  • The two standing header actions stay two.

Load-bearing files

  • lib/services/tasks.ts — searchTasksByTitle is the ancestor, not the implementation. Leave it; the link picker still needs a title-only search.
  • app/(authed)/notes/[id]/note-editor.tsx — html: false stays. The mark’s own serializer is the hatch.
  • lib/schemas/color.ts — the nine domain slugs stay. Ink adds accent and error at the ink layer, not as new domain slots.
  • components/color-swatch-picker.tsx — visual language to copy, not a component to reuse on a bubble (it is a form fieldset).
  • #main is the scroller. The ticks spy on that, not window.

08 Two things to confirm

Everything else is locked. These two change the build if you disagree. The recommendation is the locked value in the table above.

O1Is v1 really only tasks and notes?

Journal, quotes, people and links are all findable in principle. Adding them is a group in the palette and a query in the service. The cost is a fatter empty-recents state and a ranking that has to mean something across four kinds.

Recommend: tasks + notes only. You lose things in those two. Quotes you reread from Today. People you open from a mention. Grow the corpus after you have used Find for a week.

O2Colour on a phone — ship it, or desk-only?

iOS selection handles and a bubble menu share one thumb. If the first tap dismisses the native selection, colour becomes a fight, not a feature. Desk is the design-leading scene (PRODUCT.md).

Recommend: build the bubble for desk. If the phone pass is decent, keep it. If it is not, do not mount below lg and do not invent a second UI. Colour remains in the stored markdown either way, so a phone still renders it.
